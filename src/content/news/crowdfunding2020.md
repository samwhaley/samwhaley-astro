The Waszp is the fastest growing foiling dinghy in the world, and offers world class competitive racing. I have been lucky enough to have been able to learn and develop my foiling skills onboard the family Waszp for the past 3 years, and attended my first Waszp event ever in May 2019, the UK Nationals, where I finished 4th overall.

Foiling is pretty new in dinghies but is already being branded as the future of sailing. With the upcoming America's Cup now raced in foiling monohulls, I want to put myself on the forefront of foiling and ensure that I am one of the first choices for foiling expertise in the UK. That is why I believe I need to get as much world class Waszp sailing completed as possible.
