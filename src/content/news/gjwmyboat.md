###### What is MyBoat?
MyBoat is a powerful, easy-to-use, digital organiser for your vessel, from leading marine insurance company GJW Direct. It is a great place to store all your boat’s documentation together and ensures there is no scrabbling around trying to find the right bits of paperwork. Full of useful features, such a equipment maintenance alerts, it means your boat is always up to date and ready to sail with no nasty surprises!

[![MyBoat-Small-Add-V2 (002)](/uploads/MyBoat-Small-Add-V2_%28002%29_5b8241ae85.png)](https://myboat.gjwdirect.com/)

###### How useful is it when you are a boat owner? 
The MyBoat platform has been designed to make your life easier and allow you to spend as much time as possible out on the water, with less time faffing around sorting things out. 
One of the most useful features of the platform has got to be the equipment maintenance reminders, which remind you when items such as life jackets and winches need a bit of a check over. This friendly reminder means you’ll never forget to get them checked over and allows you to get out on the water safe in the knowledge that these items are in tip top condition. 

###### I have a dinghy and a jetski; can I use MyBoat for both? 
Of course. You can add any boat you like, even ones not insured by GJW Direct (although they could probably do you a better deal on that one anyway!). All you have to do is head over to the View All Vessels page to add another.

![Golden_Buoy_x_Sam_Whaley-02](/uploads/Golden_Buoy_x_Sam_Whaley-02_e8b8ad1524.jpeg)

###### I keep forgetting when my lifejackets need servicing, can I set up reminders for it?
In my opinion this is one of the best features about the MyBoat platform. Simply head over to the My Vessels page and then click on Boating Equipment to add a new reminder.

###### Don’t want email updates when these items are about to head out of date? Go to Email Options tab on the Personal Details page.

###### What kind of discounts are available in MyBoat? 
Ok possibly the coolest part of this is the range of discounts you get from being a MyBoat member. Almost like being in an exclusive members discount club, you have access to discounts at a massive range of retailers – in fact at the time of writing there are 26 separate discounts available. 
Ok yes, you also have to be a GJW insurance policy holder to unlock these discounts, but it still is a great deal.

![Golden_Buoy_x_Sam_Whaley-19](/uploads/Golden_Buoy_x_Sam_Whaley-19_d00cbeae6a.jpeg)

###### Is creating an account free? Do I have to be a GJW Direct customer to create one? 
Creating a MyBoat account is perfectly free and you do not have to be a GJW Direct customer to have an account (although they could probably offer you a great price should you wish to renew with them).

###### How easy is it to create an account? 
It is super easy to create an account and you can be up and running in under 2 minutes. It is so easy you can even personalise your dashboard to display your local weather forecast, pictures of your boats and more. 

###### Where can I go to create my MyBoat account?
Simply head over to the GJW Direct MyBoat website (https://myboat.gjwdirect.com) where you can easily log in or register for an account.

**Happy sailing.**
