Below: Opening the show on Saturday morning with Sarah, the British Sailing Team and a host of excited fans.
![IMG_3801](/uploads/IMG_3801_93b5f870fd.jpeg)

A world of opportunity - see where dinghy sailing can take you with myself, Hannah Shelmerdine and Sarah Ayton OBE.

And finally, closing off the show, the prize-giving for the kids treasure hunt.
