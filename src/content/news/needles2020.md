Last week I had the super cool experience of Waszping to the Needles, on the Isle of Wight, with a small group of friends. We set off from Lymington and sailed all the way up the Solent up to the Needles, before sailing around there for a bit and heading home.

![strava](/uploads/strava_5741e4802e.jpeg)
