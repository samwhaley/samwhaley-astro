Pleased to announce that at the start of the month I managed to win the RS Aero 7 Nationals for the 3rd time. This year it was held on the sea in Eastbourne, near Brighton and we had 4 days of brilliant sailing in some very shifty, offshore conditions.

![IMG_7167](/uploads/IMG_7167_2f3b100d44.jpeg)

Eastbourne put on a great event and the club were very friendly and accommodating with social distancing measures in place to make sure everyone was safe and happy.
