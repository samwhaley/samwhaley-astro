Tomorrow I head off to Mexico for the biggest competition of the year - the world championships. Starting on the 23rd of May, this six day regatta will see me competing against 150 of the world’s best sailors in identical, supplied boats. 

It'll be a massive physical and mental challenge but I'm really looking forward to getting out there and putting all my training to good use. 

Follow the event on the official website here: 
***[https://2022ilca7men.ilca-worlds.org/](https://2022ilca7men.ilca-worlds.org/)***

See you on the other side!
