Long time no speak. Just over a year has flown by and I am slightly behind with my goal of doing one blog post a month 😂

It's been a tricky year which firstly started off with the build up to the Paris 2024 Olympics and my role in supporting Micky in his quest for an Olympic Gold medal. It was intense, rewarding but ultimately ended up fruitless when the Olympics didn't quite go Micky's way and he missed out on a medal.

Starting off in Palma we had a stunning day for an Ovington photo shoot:
