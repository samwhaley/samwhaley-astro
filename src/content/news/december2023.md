Hello blog. Long time no see!

Been a while since I found the time to sit down and write about the past six months. With Micky being selected for the Olympics late last year, my focus has shifted into more of a training partner role, where I want to soak up as much knowledge as possible and help push him to win a gold at this year's Olympic Games.

September – November 2023 was a brilliant building fitness block, with many hours under my belt on the bike and in the gym back home in Weymouth. Coupled with a few Vilamoura training camps leading up to December made it a very productive period and I had a half-decent regatta to finish off the year at the Vilamoura GP, ending up 6th overall.
