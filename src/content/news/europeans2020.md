Pleased to announce that I finished 24th at the senior Europeans last week, held in Gdansk, Poland. It was a really tough event, especially after a breakage on day one put me on the back foot for the rest of the week, but I am happy with how I recovered to sail a consistent series and smash in a 4th in the final race to seal a place in the top 25.
