Very pleased to announce that last week I finished 11th at the ILCA 7 (Laser) World Championships out in Mexico.

![5](/uploads/5_9d89baa57b.jpeg)

By far the hardest week of my entire life, I got sick a few days into the regatta and it turned into an internal battle with myself to everyday try to perform at a respectable level. By the last day I was completely broken - but I forced myself to hang in there. Now looking back, it makes the reward of 11th overall/out of 126 competitors even sweeter.

![2022results](/uploads/2022results_44e121d9f2.png)

![3](/uploads/3_1ea14b39a9.jpeg)

Despite the circumstances, I’m really happy with my sailing last week - and over the last few months to be fair. It’s been an absolute blast to yacht around among the best in the world and great to make a step up in the senior fleet.🌍
As usual a huge thanks must go out to everyone at British Sailing Team for enabling this to happen as well as all my brilliant sponsors who have been with me at every step of the journey, especially Actisense & SailingFast💪🏼
